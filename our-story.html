Who is Sandy's, anyway?

A tribute to someone who mattered, and a name that says exactly what this crew stands for.

Who is Sandy?

A tribute to his grandfather.

The name “Sandy's” is a heartfelt tribute to Eric Sandoval's late grandfather, who played a significant role in his life.

Sandy was one of the many nicknames given to his grandfather, and by using this name, Eric honors his memory and the strong bond they shared. The name also reflects the values of the business — the kind of steady, straightforward presence Eric grew up looking up to.

In Eric's words

Eric started it to do the opposite.

Hello! My name is Eric Sandoval, and I am the proud owner of Sandy's Heating and Air. After spending 14 years working in the HVAC industry for other companies, I decided to start my own business to provide honest, reliable, and trustworthy HVAC services to the people of La Vista, Papillion, Omaha, and the surrounding areas.

My goal is simple: to deliver high-quality heating and air conditioning services without the unnecessary upselling that often burdens homeowners.
