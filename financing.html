Financing FAQ

Common questions.

What financing is available?

We offer financing through the Wells Fargo Home Projects credit card, with 0% APR options available for qualifying purchases, with approved credit.

Do I have to finance to get a discount?

No — you can pay cash or check and get 10% off your HVAC replacement instead. It's your choice, not a package deal.

Is there a fee or obligation to apply?

No. Applying online takes a few minutes and doesn't obligate you to move forward. If it's not the right fit, we'll help you find what works instead.

What can financing cover?

Heating and cooling replacements, system upgrades, and eligible repairs. Ask us if your specific job qualifies.
